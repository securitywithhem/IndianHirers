# Indian Hirers

**Indian Hirers** is a static, mobile-first web application designed to establish a credible online presence for a 25-year-old family business that rents crockery, utensils, and event supplies to hotels and caterers. The focus is on founder storytelling, trust building, and facilitating effortless inquiries without e-commerce or booking functionalities.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Build a credible online presence reflecting 25 years of heritage.
- Drive high-quality inquiries from hotel banquet managers, caterers, and event planners.
- Enable frictionless contact via click-to-call, WhatsApp, and simple inquiry forms.
- Differentiate from transactional competitors by showcasing the founder’s story.

## 🚀 Features (MVP)

- **Homepage**: Hero message, trust badges, and featured category cards.
- **Founder's Story**: Dedicated page highlighting the business's history, photos, and quotes.
- **Products**: Grid display of product categories (images only, no pricing).
- **Gallery**: Real event setups showcasing the supplies in action.
- **Testimonials**: Dedicated page and homepage section highlighting customer reviews.
- **Contact**: Inquiry form, click-to-call, WhatsApp integration, and embedded map.
- **Accessibility & Performance**: Mobile-first, app-like experience with Lighthouse performance score ≥ 95, WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility, and SEO metadata on every page.
