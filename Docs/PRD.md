# Indian Hires – Product Requirements Document (PRD)

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 18 July 2026

### Executive Summary
Indian Hires is a 25-year-old family business renting crockery, utensils, and everything an event needs to hotels and caterers. The website will be a static, mobile-first, app-like experience focused on founder storytelling, trust building, and effortless inquiry. No e‑commerce or booking is planned for this version.

### Business Objectives
- Build a credible online presence that reflects 25 years of heritage.
- Drive high-quality enquiries from hotel banquet managers, caterers, and event planners.
- Enable frictionless contact via click-to-call, WhatsApp, and a simple inquiry form.
- Differentiate from transactional competitors by showcasing the founder’s story.

### Target Audience
- **Primary:** Hotel F&B managers, event managers, catering company owners.
- **Secondary:** Wedding planners, corporate event organisers.
- **Tertiary:** Individuals hosting large private events.

### Functional Requirements (MVP)
- FR1: Homepage with hero message, trust badges, and featured category cards.
- FR2: Founder’s Story page with photos, quotes, and history.
- FR3: Products page with category grid (images only, no pricing).
- FR4: Gallery page showing real event setups.
- FR5: Testimonials (homepage section + dedicated page).
- FR6: Contact page with inquiry form, click‑to‑call, WhatsApp, embedded map.
- FR7: Floating WhatsApp button on all pages.
- FR8: Mobile bottom sticky bar (Call / WhatsApp / Enquire).
- FR9: Fully responsive, Lighthouse performance ≥ 95.
- FR10: SEO metadata on every page.

### Non‑Functional Requirements
- NFR1: Mobile‑first, app‑like feel.
- NFR2: Page load < 2s on 3G.
- NFR3: WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility.
- NFR4: Easy to maintain without a developer.
- NFR5: Zero infrastructure cost (static hosting).

### Out of Scope
- Inventory management, real‑time availability.
- Booking system, payment gateway.
- Admin dashboard.

### Success Metrics
- Monthly WhatsApp & phone inquiries.
- Contact form submission rate.
- Average time on Founders page.
- Returning visitor percentage.
