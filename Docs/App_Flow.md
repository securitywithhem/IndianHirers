# Indian Hires – App Flow (User Flow & Sitemap)

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 18 July 2026

### Sitemap
```
Home (/) 
  ├── Our Story (/founders)
  ├── What We Rent (/products)
  ├── Gallery (/gallery)
  ├── Testimonials (/testimonials)
  └── Contact (/contact)
```

### Primary Flow – Caterer Enquiry
1. Land on Home → see hero & trust badges.
2. Browse Products → tap category.
3. Tap WhatsApp → chat directly → receive quote.
Or: click Call / fill Contact form.

### Secondary Flow – Hotel Manager (First-time)
1. Search → Home.
2. Read testimonials → visit Founders page.
3. Trust established → submit inquiry form.

### Tertiary Flow – Individual Host
1. Social referral → Gallery.
2. See event photos → Products → WhatsApp.

### Navigation & CTAs
- All pages reachable from header/footer.
- Product cards alert “Full catalogue coming soon – WhatsApp us!”
- Mobile bottom bar + floating WhatsApp always visible.

### Edge Cases
- Form validation errors inline.
- Loading state on submit.
- Custom 404 page with link home.
